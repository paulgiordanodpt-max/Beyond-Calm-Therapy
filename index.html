Conditions We Help Support

Many chronic symptoms involve a sensitive nervous system, changes in pain processing, stress responses, sleep disruption, and movement avoidance. Our approach focuses on education, nervous system regulation, movement confidence, and symptom management.

Restless Legs Symptoms

Strategies to improve symptom management, sleep quality, movement confidence, and nervous system regulation.

Chronic Radiculopathy

Support for persistent nerve-related symptoms including discomfort, tingling, and sensitivity that continue beyond expected healing timelines.

Neuralgias

Education and symptom-management strategies for individuals experiencing persistent nerve sensitivity and nerve-related pain.

Complex Regional Pain Syndrome (CRPS)

A gradual, nervous-system-informed approach focused on movement confidence, desensitization, function, and quality of life.

Fibromyalgia

Support for individuals experiencing widespread pain, fatigue, sleep disruption, and nervous system sensitivity.

Migraines & Chronic Tension Headaches

Education, movement strategies, stress management, and nervous system regulation to help reduce symptom burden and improve daily function.


Services focus on education, coaching, movement-based wellness, and symptom management. We do not diagnose, treat, or cure medical conditions.
