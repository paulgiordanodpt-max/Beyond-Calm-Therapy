What Restless Legs Feels Like

People experiencing restless legs symptoms often describe an uncomfortable urge to move the legs, especially at night or during rest. Symptoms may feel worse when lying still and may temporarily improve with movement.
This can contribute to difficulty falling asleep, frequent nighttime awakenings, and increased frustration or anxiety around bedtime.

Why Restless Legs Symptoms May Persist

Restless legs symptoms are complex and may involve multiple contributing factors. One perspective considers the role of nervous system sensitivity, where the body becomes more reactive to internal sensations, stress, and stillness.
This heightened sensitivity can create a cycle where attention, discomfort, and sleep disruption reinforce one another over time.

Why Traditional Approaches Don’t Always Provide Lasting Relief

Many people try stretching, supplements, massage, or medications. While these may provide temporary relief, symptoms often return because the underlying sensitivity and movement perception patterns remain unchanged.
