Could Your Symptoms Be Driven by Nervous System Sensitization?

This educational quiz is not a medical diagnosis. It is designed to help identify patterns often associated with nervous system sensitization and chronic symptom amplification.

1. Have medical tests failed to identify a clear cause for your symptoms?


2. Do your symptoms worsen during stressful periods?


3. Do your symptoms fluctuate significantly from day to day?


4. Does poor sleep make your symptoms noticeably worse?


5. Have treatments focused only on the body provided limited long-term relief?


6. Do you find yourself constantly monitoring or thinking about your symptoms?


7. Are your symptoms worse when sitting still or trying to relax?


8. Have symptoms spread or changed over time?
